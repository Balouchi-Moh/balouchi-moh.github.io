SERVICES

Training and planning-process support built on LPS

Remote services for contractors and developers in the GCC: a Weekly Planning Process Review, Last Planner® System training and setup support.

01

Weekly Planning Process Review

A one-week, fixed-fee remote review of how your site plans its week: the weekly work plan, constraint log, look-ahead, PPC and the planning meetings. You send the documents you already use; I review them against Last Planner® System practice and tell you what to fix first.

You get: a 6–10 page written report · prioritized list of fixes · 30-minute clarification call · 60-minute presentation session.

Scope note: this is a review of the planning process. It is not a technical audit of a Primavera P6 file.

02

Remote Last Planner® System Training — 1 to 2 days

An online course for planners, site engineers and project managers: pull planning, look-ahead and make-ready, constraint logs, weekly work plans, PPC and learning from variances. Exercises use your own project where possible. Executive training on PMBOK is also available.

You get: tailored course program · templates for the weekly plan and constraint log · practical exercises · short follow-up Q&A.

03

LPS Setup Support

After a review or training, remote support while your team sets up its own weekly planning routine: templates, meeting format and PPC reporting. Your team runs the system; I coach and review. Site visits only on request, with travel costs covered by the client.

You get: agreed scope and schedule · regular remote sessions · review of your weekly plans and PPC · written recommendations.
