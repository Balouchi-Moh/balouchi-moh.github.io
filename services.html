ADVISORY & CONSULTING SERVICES

Professional advisory for contractors, developers and investors

Tailored engineering and management solutions for clients across the GCC and Middle East — delivered remotely, on a retainer, or through short on-site engagements.

01

Lean Construction & Last Planner® System (LPS) Implementation

Full deployment of collaborative site planning: reliable weekly commitments, PPC tracking, phase scheduling and root-cause variance analysis — to eliminate workflow bottlenecks and prevent delays before they happen. Includes training of site teams and setup of the weekly cadence your planners run themselves.

You get: LPS rollout plan · trained planners · weekly PPC routine · variance reports your management can act on.

02

Takt Time Planning & Workflow Optimization

Structuring repetitive high-rise, residential and industrial construction into continuous, synchronized production sequences — zones, takt time and trade handover rules that stabilize the site and make the completion date a matter of arithmetic, not hope.

You get: takt zone plan · synchronized trade schedule · location-based progress tracking.

03

Project Controls & Primavera P6

Primavera P6 baseline schedule development, progress monitoring, Earned Value Management (EVM) and reporting systems — a clean, maintainable P6 setup your team can actually keep alive, with the KPIs management needs for decisions.

You get: P6 baseline & WBS structure · progress measurement system · EVM reports · updated scheduling procedures.

04

Forensic Delay Analysis & Claim Management

Research-grade forensic schedule analysis to identify, quantify and allocate delay — plus professional claim documentation and dispute mitigation under FIDIC-based contracts. Grounded in 15 years of contract-level experience on megaprojects.

You get: delay analysis report (TIA / windows) · claim narrative & exhibits · negotiation support.

05

Lean Training & Executive Workshops

Focused workshops and on-site programs: Last Planner® System rollout, takt planning, lean fundamentals and project-controls training — for planning teams, execution teams and executives. Delivered in English, Persian or Arabic.

You get: tailored workshop program · practical exercises on your own project data · follow-up coaching.
