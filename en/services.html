ADVISORY & CONSULTING SERVICES

Professional advisory for contractors, developers and investors

Tailored engineering and management solutions for clients across the GCC and Middle East — delivered remotely, on a retainer, or through short on-site engagements.

1 · Lean Construction & Last Planner® System (LPS) Implementation

Full deployment of collaborative site planning, reliable commitment tracking (PPC), phase scheduling and root-cause variance analysis — to eliminate workflow bottlenecks and prevent project delays. Includes training of site teams and weekly cadence setup.

2 · Takt Time Planning & Workflow Optimization

Structuring repetitive high-rise, residential and industrial construction into continuous, synchronized production sequences — to stabilize trade handovers and achieve predictable completion dates.

3 · Project Controls, Delay Analysis & Claim Management

Primavera P6 baseline schedule development, progress monitoring, Earned Value Management (EVM), forensic schedule delay analysis and dispute mitigation — grounded in 15 years of contract-level experience on megaprojects.

4 · Real Estate Valuation & Technical Due Diligence

Comprehensive technical appraisal, feasibility evaluation and asset valuation conducted to international standards — for developers and institutional investors entering the market.
